# 🍽️ Continuous Glucose Monitoring (CGM) & Diet Analysis  

## 📌 Overview  
This project analyzed the relationship between **dietary intake, lifestyle activity, gut microbiome composition, and glucose variability** using **Continuous Glucose Monitoring (CGM) data** combined with diet logs and activity records.  

The goal was to **classify diet and lifestyle quality, evaluate postprandial glucose responses, and assess gut health**, providing insights for **precision nutrition** and **diabetes management**.  

---

## 🎯 Objectives  
- Integrate **CGM, diet, lifestyle activity, and gut microbiome data** for comprehensive analysis.  
- Categorize participants by **food habits (healthy vs poor)** and **activity level (sedentary, active, vigorous)**.  
- Evaluate **gut microbiome health** based on gut lining integrity and microbial counts (beneficial, harmful, opportunistic bacteria).  
- Deliver **interactive Power BI dashboards** showcasing dietary quality, glucose outcomes, and gut health indicators.  

---

## 🛠 Tools & Skills  
- **PostgreSQL** – Accessed and queried participant datasets.  
- **Power BI** – Data cleaning, transformation, modeling, and dashboard development with DAX.  
- **Python (os, pandas)** – Automated renaming and organization of **3,577 participant food menu images**.  
- **Google Apps Script** – Created Excel files linking food images with online URLs for structured referencing.  
- **MS Excel** – Organized and analyzed gut microbiome datasets.  

---

## 📊 Key Features  
- Built a **nutrition and lifestyle classification system** combining macronutrient intake, fiber levels, food habits, and activity levels (sedentary, active, vigorous).  
- Processed and structured **gut microbiome data** to categorize participants’ gut health as *Healthy*, *Poor*, or *Balanced* based on microbial composition.  
- Developed **Power BI dashboards** visualizing diet quality, glucose response, lifestyle activity patterns, and gut microbiome associations.  
- Applied **data storytelling** to highlight connections between dietary habits, gut health, and glucose variability.
